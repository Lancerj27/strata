# Sounds
@screen AssetMasterScreen:Sound
Where: Options > Sounds.

Every sound the game knows, grouped by mod and folder: mobs, blocks, items, music, ambient and the rest.
Replace a game sound with one of yours, for every mob, block or screen that plays it.

What you can do here
- Click a sound to play it. Play plays it again.
- Search finds a sound by its id.
- Replace... puts one of your custom sounds in its place. + Add custom... in the picker imports a new one.
- Restore puts the game's sound back.
- A replaced sound shows in yellow.

Replacing against one mob
To change the sound of one mob or variant alone, use Mob data > Sounds in its editor. Here changes the sound for everything that plays it.

Step by step: a softer creeper
1. Search "creeper.primed".
2. Replace... Pick your quiet hiss, or + Add custom... to import one.
3. Click the sound to hear it.

Examples
- Your own music for the title screen: music.menu.
- A silent cave: the ambient.cave sounds replaced with a silent .ogg.
- A new level-up chime: entity.player.levelup.

Replacements are saved in the profile. Load active profile to server (in Profiles) sends them to every player.
