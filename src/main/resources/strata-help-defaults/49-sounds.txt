# Sounds
@screen AssetMasterScreen:Sound
Where: Options > Sounds.

Every sound the game knows, grouped by mod and folder.

What you can do here
- Click a sound to play it. Play plays it again.
- Replace... puts one of your custom sounds in its place.
- Restore puts the game's sound back.

Examples
- A softer creeper hiss.
- Your own music for the title screen.

Replacements are saved in the profile. Load active profile to server (in Profiles) sends them to every player.
