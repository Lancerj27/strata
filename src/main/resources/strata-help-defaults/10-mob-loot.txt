# Mob loot
@screen LootScreen
@screen LootEntryScreen
Where: the mob editor, Loot.

What the mob drops, at this scope, on top of its own loot table.

The list
- The game's drops first, from the mob's loot table, then your entries.
- + Add loot entry adds an item with its chance and count. Your item variants are listed first.
- Edit opens an entry. Remove takes it out.
- Remove on one of the game's drops denies it here. Restore brings it back.
- Loot table opens every value of the mob's own loot table: rolls, items, weights, counts, looting bonus and chances.
- Restore all clears this scope's changes.

An entry
- Item and count: a number, or a range like 1-3.
- Chance %: how often it drops.
- Condition: Random chance, Looting level, Player level (a number, a range like 3-10, or a minimum like 5+), and the rest.
- Looting bonus: extra count per level of Looting.

Scopes
An entry at Global drops everywhere. An entry on a tier drops in the tier's biomes. An entry on a biome drops there only. The scopes add up: a zombie in a biome drops its Global, tier and biome entries.

Step by step: emeralds from hard zombies
1. World > Mobs. Open the hard tier's tab. Edit the zombie.
2. Loot. + Add loot entry. Emerald. Count 1. Chance 5.
3. Save in Profiles.

Examples
- Creepers stop dropping gunpowder in one biome: open the biome's tab, edit the creeper, Loot, Remove on gunpowder.
- A boss variant that always drops a diamond: Chance 100.
- Skeletons that drop more arrows: Loot table, raise the arrow count.

When it applies
- Your loot entries: at once.
- Loot table values: from the next world load.
