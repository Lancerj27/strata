# Mob loot
@screen LootScreen
@screen LootEntryScreen
Where: the mob editor, Loot.

What the mob drops, at this scope, on top of its own loot table.

What you can do here
- + Add loot entry adds an item with its chance and count.
- Remove on one of the game's drops denies it here.
- Loot table opens every value of the mob's own loot table: rolls, items, weights, counts, looting bonus and chances.
- Remove takes entries out. Restore all clears this scope's changes.

Examples
- Zombies drop an emerald 5% of the time in a hard tier.
- Creepers stop dropping gunpowder in one biome.
- A boss variant that always drops a diamond.

When it applies
- Your loot entries: at once.
- Loot table values: from the next world load.
