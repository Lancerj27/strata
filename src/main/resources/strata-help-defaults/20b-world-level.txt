# World level
@screen WorldLevelScreen
Where: World > World level.

The world climbs a ladder of stages as its players progress. A stage is a tab. Each stage says what reaches it,
what stays locked until then, and how much harder mobs spawn.

The screen
- Tabs: Start, then each stage by its name. + Stage adds one to the right. Arrows page the tabs.
- Under the tabs: the stage's form. Change it, then Save stage.
- Over the list: the rule row. Fill it, then + Add rule.
- The list: the stage's rules. Edit loads one into the row. Remove takes out the picked ones.
- Remove stage takes out the open stage with its rules.

How to: make mobs grow with the players
1. Open Start. Set Coefficient to 0.1. Save stage.
2. Every mob now spawns one level higher for every ten XP levels the top player has.
3. Want it to follow the players down too? Set Level source to highest_online.

How to: unlock the iron pickaxe at a stage
1. + Stage. Name it "Iron". Save stage.
2. In the rule row pick lock_recipe, click the value button, pick Iron Pickaxe. + Add rule.
3. Pick xp_level, type 10. + Add rule.
4. Until a player reaches XP 10, the iron pickaxe recipe makes nothing.

How to: a stage that switches off again
1. On the stage set Hold to while_met. Save stage.
2. Give it xp_level 20 as a condition.
3. The stage is on while the top player is at XP 20 or more, and off again below it.

How to: a dangerous region before its time
1. On a stage pick lock_tier, choose the tier, click Effect and pick Weakness, Amplifier 1. + Add rule.
2. Players in that tier get Weakness II every second until the stage is reached.

The stage form
- Name: told to the players when the stage is reached. It is the tab's label.
- Players: any, one player meeting a condition counts. all, every player online must.
- Conditions: all, every condition must be met. any, one is enough.
- Offset: flat levels put on every spawn. 2 means two levels higher, whoever is playing.
- Coefficient: levels per player level. 0.1 is one level per ten. 0.25 is one per four.
- Mode: how this stage changes the totals. See The modes.
- Hold: keep, a reached stage stays. while_met, it holds only while its conditions hold now.
- Level source (Start only): whose level the coefficient uses. See Level source.

The modes
- The world keeps two totals: an offset and a coefficient. Start sets them. Every spawn gains the total offset
  plus the total coefficient times the player level.
- add: the stage puts its offset and coefficient on the totals. Start 0.25 plus a stage adding 0.05 is 0.30.
- subtract: the stage takes them off.
- multiply: the stage scales the totals by its numbers. Coefficient 2 doubles the total.
- scale_level: the totals are left alone. Every spawn level is multiplied by 1 + offset + coefficient x player
  level. Here 0.5 is "half again" and 0.01 is "one percent per level". Use it for a percentage boost.

Level source (on Start)
- highest_ever: the highest XP level any player has ever reached. Never falls.
- highest_online: the highest XP level among the players online now.
- average_online: their average.
- total_online: their XP levels added up.

Conditions
- advancement: a player earned it.
- xp_level: a player's XP level reached the number.
- tier, biome, dimension, structure: a player entered it.
- area_level: a player entered an area whose Strata level is the number or more.
- item: a player carried the item.
- kill_entity: a player killed that kind of mob.
- mob_kill_level: a player killed a mob of that Strata level or more.
- kill_count: a player's kills reached the number.
- days: the world's day reached the number.

Locks
- lock_block: the block does not break. A tag works too, typed as #minecraft:logs in world_stage_rules.csv.
- lock_recipe: the recipe makes nothing.
- lock_item: the item does nothing when used.
- lock_tier, lock_biome, lock_dimension, lock_structure: being there gives the effect, at its amplifier.
- lock_area_level: areas of that Strata level or more give the effect.
- Locks are silent. Creative players break and use anything; a locked recipe makes nothing for them too.

Where it is kept
- The stages and rules are in the profile: world_stages.csv and world_stage_rules.csv.
- The stage reached and each player's progress are saved in the world. A new world starts at Start.

Commands
- /strata stage: the stage, the world level and the player level figure.
- /strata stage <n>: sets the stage (operator). A while_met stage settles again on the next second.

More scaling (Options > Settings)
- World stage scale: levels per stage reached, on top of the totals.
- Player level scale: levels per XP level of the nearest player, so each player's own surroundings.
- Scaling > Formulas > Area level names player_level, world_stage and world_level too.
