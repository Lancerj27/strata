# Structures
@screen OverridesScreen:structures
@screen CustomStructuresScreen
@screen CustomStructureScreen
Where: World > Structures.

Every structure of the game and its mods, grouped by type, with their spacing, pieces, weathering, modifiers and tags.

What you can do here
- Edit a structure's biomes and the mobs that spawn inside it.
- Spacing: how far apart its set places it (spacing, separation, salt).
- Off: it is never placed again.
- Custom structures: your own .nbt builds, placed by the world like a village.

Custom structures
- Save a build with a structure block, then type the path of the .nbt file.
- At most 48 blocks on each side, and 1 MB.
- Biomes: a biome tag or biome ids. Spacing and Separation in chunks. Land around it: none, beard_thin, beard_box, bury or encapsulate. Height: surface, or a Y level.

Examples
- Villages twice as far apart: raise their spacing.
- No ocean ruins: Off.
- Your own tower in every forest, one in about 40 chunks.
- Witches and more spawn inside swamp huts: edit its spawns.

When it applies: in land made after the next world load. Land already made keeps what it has.
