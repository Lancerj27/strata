# Item variants
@screen ItemVariantScreen
@screen ItemVariantStatsScreen
Where: Items > Items, + Add variant under an item.

Your own items, made from a base item.

What you can do here
- The look: display name, lore, rarity, glint, unbreakable, custom model data.
- Stats: the base item's numbers, yours in yellow. + Add attribute and + Add enchantment add more.
- Effects: abilities while worn or held, on deal damage or on take damage.
- Use it anywhere an item is picked: mob equipment, loot, chest loot and trades. Variants are listed first.
- /strata item give <player> <variant> [count] gives one.

Examples
- "Frostbite", an iron sword that slows what it hits.
- "Miner's Helmet", a helmet that gives night vision.
- A rare loot sword with Sharpness 6.

When it applies: at once. Stacks already made get the change too.
