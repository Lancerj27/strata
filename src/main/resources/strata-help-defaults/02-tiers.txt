# Tiers
@screen TierListScreen
@screen TierEditScreen
@screen RulesScreen
Where: World > Tiers.

A tier is a group of biomes that share one level, one mob list and one set of rules.
Use tiers to make regions of the world easier or harder.

What you can do here
- + Add tier makes a new tier. Duplicate copies the selected tier.
- Edit opens a tier: its name, level, biomes and settings.
- + Add biomes opens the biomes with no tier. Click one to add it, or Add shown to add every biome the search shows.
- Spawn mode: Add puts the tier's mobs on top of the game's spawns. Replace uses only the tier's list.
- Scale mobs, Substitute spawns and Restrict ores switch those parts on or off for the tier.
- Coefficient multiplies the scaling bonus of every mob in the tier.
- Mobs, Blocks and Structures open the tier's own lists.
- Rules holds the tier's spawn switches. Select one, then On, Off or Default.

The rules:
- Replace blocks other spawns.
- Monsters take creature, ambient and water slots.
- Monsters spawn outside of pure darkness.
- Special mob spawns.
A biome's own value wins over its tier's. With neither, the rule is on.

Examples
- A "Deep" tier at level 30 for the deep dark and the dripstone caves: mobs there spawn stronger.
- A Replace tier for the Nether wastes that spawns only your mob list.
- Coefficient 2.0 on an end-game tier doubles the scaling bonus there.

When it applies
- Level, coefficient and scaling: at once.
- Spawn lists and ore rules: in land made after the next world restart.
- The slot rules: from the next world load. The other rules: at once.
