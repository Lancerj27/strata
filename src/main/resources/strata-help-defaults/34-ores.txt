# Ores
@screen OreListScreen
Where: Blocks > Ores, or the Blocks button of a tier or biome.

Every ore, on or off, with its chance.
The Global list is under every tier and biome. A biome's rule wins over its tier's, a tier's over Global.

What you can do here
- On / Off: the ore generates there or not.
- Type a chance to make it more or less common.

Examples
- No diamonds above the deep tier.
- Twice the coal in a mountain tier.

When it applies: in land made after the next world load.
