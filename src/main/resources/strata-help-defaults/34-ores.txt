# Ores
@screen OreListScreen
Where: Blocks > Ores, or the Blocks button of a tier or biome.

Every ore of the game and its mods, on or off, with its chance.
The Global list is under every tier and biome. A biome's rule wins over its tier's, a tier's over Global.

The list
- One row per ore. Columns show its state and chance at this scope, and where the value comes from.
- On / Off: the ore generates there or not.
- Default: the row takes the scope beneath (a tier takes Global, a biome its tier).
- Type a chance to make it more or less common. 100 is the game's amount. 50 is half. 200 is double.
- Search finds an ore by name or id.
- Ore restriction in Options > Settings switches this whole part on or off.

Scopes
- Global: the whole world. Turn an ore Off here and it is gone everywhere, unless a tier turns it On.
- A tier: its biomes. A deep tier with diamonds On and a surface tier with diamonds Off.
- A biome: that biome alone.

Step by step: diamonds only in the deep
1. Blocks > Ores. Diamond ore, Off.
2. World > Tiers. Edit the "Deep" tier. Blocks. Ores tab. Diamond ore, On.
3. Save in Profiles. New chunks carry the change.

Examples
- Twice the coal in a mountain tier: coal ore, chance 200.
- No ancient debris anywhere: Off on Global.
- A modded ore only in one biome: Off on Global, On on the biome.

For the exact Y range, vein size and count, see Features.
An ore turned off becomes the stone around it. A regenerate (Profiles) applies it to old chunks too.

When it applies: in land made after the next world load.
