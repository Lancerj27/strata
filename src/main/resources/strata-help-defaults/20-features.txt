# Features
@screen OverridesScreen:features
Where: World > Features.

What biomes place: ores, trees, lakes, geodes, flowers. Grouped by the biome that places them.
A feature several biomes place is listed under each. A change changes it everywhere.

What you can do here
- An ore's lowest and highest Y, times per chunk and rarity.
- Settings (the button beside Edit): vein size and the block it places.
- Off: it is placed zero times.

Examples
- Iron veins of 20 blocks.
- Diamonds from Y -64 to Y 32.
- No amethyst geodes: Off.

When it applies: in land made after the next world load.
