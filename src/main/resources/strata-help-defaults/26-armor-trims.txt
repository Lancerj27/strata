# Armor trims
@screen OverridesScreen:armor_trims
Where: Items > Data... > Armor trims.

The trim materials and trim patterns in one list, a group each.

What you can do here
- A material's item, colour and name.
- A pattern's template item and texture.

Examples
- A trim material made from your own item.
- A different colour for the copper trim.

When it applies: at the next world load or /reload.
