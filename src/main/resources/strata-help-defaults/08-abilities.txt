# Abilities
@screen AbilitiesScreen
Where: the mob editor, Abilities. Item variants (Effects) and block variants (Abilities) use the same screen.

Special powers that run on a trigger.

What you can do here
- Add an ability: apply an effect, damage, life steal, an aura, healing, an immunity, spawn a mob, explode, and more.
- Each ability has a trigger: on spawn, on death, on deal damage, on take damage, on flee, in sunlight, and the rest of the game's own triggers.
- Chance %: how often it lands when the trigger fires.
- Hover a field for what it means. 20 ticks are 1 second.
- Turn off switches one off without removing it.

Examples
- A spider that poisons on hit: Apply effect, poison, on deal damage.
- A zombie that heals 2 health every 5 seconds.
- A creeper that spawns silverfish on death.
- A block variant that burns whoever stands on it.

When it applies: at once, to every mob that has it.
