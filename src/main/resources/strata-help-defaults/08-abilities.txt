# Abilities
@screen AbilitiesScreen
Where: the mob editor, Abilities. Item variants (Effects) and block variants (Abilities) use the same screen.

Special powers that run on a trigger. A mob may hold many rows. Each row is one ability on one trigger.

The list
- + Add ability adds a row. Pick the ability, then its trigger.
- Edit opens a row. Remove deletes the picked rows.
- Turn off switches one off and keeps it. Turn on brings it back.
- Hover a field for what it means. 20 ticks are 1 second.

The abilities
- Apply effect: gives itself a game effect, with its level and length.
- Damage over time: so much damage every so often for a total time. Poison, wither, fire and bleed are all this. Can kill lets it finish the target.
- Life steal: the damage it deals heals it.
- Attribute change: raises or lowers an attribute for a time. A cure item ends it.
- Explode: damage in a radius, the same at the edge as at the center.
- Explode and die: explodes, then the mob is gone, as a creeper is. It drops nothing.
- Spawn: a creature appears beside it.
- Heal: exact health back, so much every so often.
- Aura: a game effect on the creatures near it, not on itself. One kind of creature, a #tag of them, or all but players.
- Kill: the target dies outright.
- Sun immune: daylight never burns it.
- Sun weakness: it burns in daylight under open sky, as a zombie does.
- Damage immunity: it takes no damage of one kind, a damage type or a #tag of them.

The triggers
- On spawn: once, when it joins the world.
- On death: when it dies.
- On hit: when it hurts a creature. The effect lands on the creature.
- On hurt: when a creature hurts it. The effect lands on the attacker.
- On take damage: when it is hurt. The effect lands on itself.
- On flee: when it starts to flee.
- In sunlight: while it stands in daylight.
- Permanent: all the time. With Ticks, it repeats on that timer.
- On impact: for a projectile, where it lands. See Projectiles.

The fields
- Chance %: how often it lands when the trigger fires. Blank is 100.
- Amount, Ticks, Radius, Damage, Duration: what the ability needs. A field the ability does not read stays hidden.
- Damage type, Particle, HUD icon, Cure, Entity: extras some abilities take.

Step by step: a poison spider
1. Edit the spider. Abilities.
2. + Add ability. Apply effect. Trigger on hit.
3. Effect poison. Amount 0 (level I). Duration 100 (5 seconds). Chance 100.
4. Save in Profiles. The next spider poisons on hit.

Examples
- A zombie that heals 2 health every 5 seconds: Heal, permanent, Amount 2, Ticks 100.
- A creeper that spawns silverfish on death: Spawn, on death, Entity silverfish.
- A block variant that burns whoever stands on it: Damage over time with the fire damage type.
- A helmet variant that gives night vision while worn: Apply effect, permanent, night vision.

When it applies: at once, to every mob that has it.
