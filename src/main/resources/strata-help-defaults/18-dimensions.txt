# Dimensions
@screen OverridesScreen:dimensions
Where: World > Dimensions.

The game's dimension types (Overworld, Nether, End) and the dimensions a mod or a pack adds.

What you can do here
- Select a dimension type to see its fields. Select a field, type a value, Set value. Restore puts it back.
- Height and Min Y: how tall the world is and where its bottom is. Height is a multiple of 16. Min Y plus Height is at most 2032.
- Logical height: the highest Y a portal or chorus fruit may take a player.
- Ambient light: 0 is dark nights. 0.1 is the Nether's glow.
- Has skylight, Has ceiling: the Nether has a ceiling and no skylight.
- Bed works, Respawn anchor works: whether each explodes.
- Piglin safe, Natural, Ultrawarm: zombified piglins, compass and clock, lava flow and water.
- Fixed time: a tick of the day the dimension always shows. Blank for a day cycle.
- Coordinate scale: the Nether's 8.
- Monster spawn light level: the light a monster may spawn in.
- Infiniburn: the blocks fire burns on forever.

Step by step: beds in the Nether
1. Select the Nether dimension type.
2. Bed works. Type true. Set value.
3. Make a new world, or visit Nether land not made yet.

Examples
- A taller Overworld with a lower bottom: Min Y -128, Height 512.
- Ambient light in the End: Ambient light 0.1.
- An Overworld that is always noon: Fixed time 6000.

When it applies: in a new world, or land not made yet. A height change on an old world can tear its land.
