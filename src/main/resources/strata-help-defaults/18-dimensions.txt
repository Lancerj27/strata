# Dimensions
@screen OverridesScreen:dimensions
Where: World > Dimensions.

The game's dimension types and the dimensions a mod or a pack adds.

What you can do here
- Height and lowest Y, light, ceiling, sky, and whether beds and respawn anchors work.
- Select a field, type a value, Set value. Restore puts it back.

Examples
- Beds that work in the Nether.
- A taller Overworld with a lower bottom.
- Ambient light in the End.

When it applies: in a new world, or land not made yet.
