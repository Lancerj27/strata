# Enchantments
@screen OverridesScreen:enchantments
@screen OverridesScreen:enchantment_provider
Where: Items > Data... > Enchantments, and Enchantment rolls.

The enchantments and their tags in one list, a group each.
Enchantment rolls decide what enchantments mob gear, trades and loot can get.

What you can do here
- An enchantment's max level, weight, cost, the items it goes on, and what it does.
- Enchantment tags: which enchantments are treasure, which go on tables, which clash.
- Select a field, type a value, Set value.

Examples
- Sharpness up to level 10.
- Mending on the enchanting table: add it to the in_enchanting_table tag.
- Protection that stacks with Fire Protection: remove the clash.

When it applies: at the next world load or /reload.
