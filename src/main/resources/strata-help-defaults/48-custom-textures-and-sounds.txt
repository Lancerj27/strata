# Custom textures and sounds
@screen CustomAssetsScreen
@screen CustomAssetScreen
Where: Options > Custom textures & sounds, or "+ Add custom..." in a texture or sound picker.

Your own textures, sounds and models.

What you can do here
- + Add custom...: pick the kind (Texture, Sound or Model), Browse... for the file, and type a name (lowercase letters, digits and _).
- A texture also takes a category (entity, item, block, misc). A sound can take a subtitle.
- Re-import swaps the file and keeps the name, so every mob that uses it keeps working.
- Remove deletes it.

Files
- Texture: PNG, each side 16 to 512 pixels and a multiple of 16, at most 256 KB.
- Sound: Ogg Vorbis, mono or stereo, at most 20 seconds and 1 MB. Use mono for a sound that comes from a place.
- Model: a block or item model, JSON, at most 64 KB.
- A mob's texture must fit the mob's model: keep its size and layout.

The library belongs to this machine (config/strata/assets_pack). A server sends its own files to players when they join.

Examples
- A red skin for your "Blood Zombie" variant.
- A new roar for a boss variant.

When it applies: at once.
