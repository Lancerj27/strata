# Brewing
@screen OverridesScreen:brewing
Where: Items > Data... > Brewing.

Every brewing stand mix of the game and its mods: from which potion, with which ingredient, makes which.
Bottle mixes turn a potion into a splash potion, and a splash potion into a lingering one.

What you can do here
- Change a mix's input, ingredient or output.
- Duplicate element copies a mix. Change the copy for a new mix.
- Remove element takes a mix out.

Examples
- A potion of luck from an awkward potion and an emerald.
- No potions of weakness: remove the mixes that make them.

When it applies: at the next world load.
