# Special spawns
@screen SpecialMobsScreen
@screen SpecialSpawnsScreen
Where: World > Mobs, Special spawns. Also in a tier's or biome's editor.

The game's special spawners: phantoms, pillager patrols, village sieges, the wandering trader, village cats and villagers (iron golems, breeding, type and profession).
These mobs do not come from spawn lists. Each has its own spawner with its own timing and conditions.

The list
- One group per spawner. Each value says what it does and shows the game's number.
- Select a value, type yours, Set value. Restore puts the game's number back.
- Pick... picks a mob, or a villager's type or profession. A double-click turns a switch.
- Special mob spawns Off, at the top, stops every special spawner here.
- Spawn as in the mob editor makes a mob Normal (from spawn lists) or Special (from its spawner).

The spawners
- Phantoms: the mob, how many, the sleep-less days a player needs, and the height above sea level.
- Patrols: the mob, the leader, the group size, the first day, and how often a patrol tries.
- Sieges: the mob, how many, and the chance a night brings one.
- Wandering trader: the trader, its llamas, how often it tries, and how long it stays.
- Village cats: the cat, and how many a village holds.
- Villagers: iron golem summons, breeding, the villager type by biome, and the profession by workstation.

Scopes
A biome's value wins, then its tier's, then Global's. How often each spawner tries is set on Global only.

Step by step: no phantoms in a tier
1. World > Tiers. Edit the tier. Rules. Special mob spawns Off.
2. Or: open the tier's Special spawns. Set the phantom count to 0.

Examples
- Patrols of your own variant in place of pillagers: Patrols, Pick... the mob, choose the variant.
- Phantoms as Normal mobs that spawn only where your lists put them: edit the phantom, Mob data, Spawn as Normal.
- A wandering trader every day: raise its chance on Global.
- Bigger sieges: raise the siege count.

When it applies: at once. Your first edit turns Options > Special spawners On, which takes effect at the next world load.
