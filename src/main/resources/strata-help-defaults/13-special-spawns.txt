# Special spawns
@screen SpecialMobsScreen
@screen SpecialSpawnsScreen
Where: World > Mobs, Special spawns. Also in a tier's or biome's editor.

The game's special spawners: phantoms, pillager patrols, village sieges, the wandering trader, village cats and villagers (iron golems, breeding, type and profession).

What you can do here
- Each value says what it does and shows the game's number. Set value types yours.
- Pick... picks a mob, or a villager's type or profession. A double-click turns a switch.
- Special mob spawns Off, at the top, stops every special spawner here.
- Spawn as in the mob editor makes a mob Normal (from spawn lists) or Special (from its spawner).

A biome's value wins, then its tier's, then Global's. How often each spawner tries is set on Global only.

Examples
- No phantoms in a peaceful tier: Special mob spawns Off.
- Patrols of your own variant in place of pillagers.
- Phantoms as Normal mobs that spawn only where your lists put them.

When it applies: at once. Your first edit turns Options > Special spawners On, which takes effect at the next world load.
