# Test mode
@screen TestModeScreen
Where: Options > Test mode.

Spawn a mob at a level, to see what scaling does.

What you can do here
- Pick a mob and type an area level.
- The preview shows its scaled stats.
- Spawn test mob spawns it beside you.

Examples
- A level 50 zombie, to check your curves.
- Your variant at level 1, to check its look.

/strata test spawn <mob> <level> does the same.
