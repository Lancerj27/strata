# Test mode
@screen TestModeScreen
Where: Options > Test mode.

Spawn a mob at a level, to see what scaling does. A world must be open.

What you can do here
- Pick a mob. Your variants are in the list.
- Type an area level, 0 or more.
- The preview shows its scaled stats at that level: health, damage, armor, speed and the rest, from your curves.
- Spawn test mob spawns it beside you, at that level.
- A line says why the button is off: no mob picked, no level, or not a whole number.

Step by step: check a curve
1. Scaling > Mob scaling. Edit health, per_level 0.05.
2. Options > Test mode. Zombie. Level 50.
3. The preview reads 70 health (20 x (1 + 0.05 x 50)).
4. Spawn test mob. /strata mob get beside it confirms.

Examples
- A level 50 zombie, to check your curves.
- Your variant at level 1, to check its look.
- A boss at level 100, to check its bar.

/strata test spawn <mob> <level> does the same. Operator.
