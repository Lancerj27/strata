# World settings
@screen WorldSettingsScreen
Where: Options > World settings.

The open world's own settings, changed live. A world must be open. These are the world's, not the profile's: a new world starts with the game's values.

What you can do here
- World border: center X and Z, size in blocks, damage per block outside, safe zone, warning distance and warning time.
- Difficulty: peaceful, easy, normal or hard. Difficulty locked keeps it.
- Time of day: 0 sunrise, 6000 noon, 12000 sunset, 18000 midnight.
- Day length in minutes: 20 is the game's. 40 is a slow day.
- Weather: clear, rain or thunder, and how long it lasts in ticks.
- Spawn angle: the way a new player faces.
- Save sets only what you changed.

Step by step: a bordered hard world
1. Options > World settings.
2. World border size 2000. Center X and Z at your spawn.
3. Difficulty hard. Difficulty locked true.
4. Save.

Examples
- 40-minute days.
- Clear weather for an hour: Weather clear, 72000.
- A sunrise right now: Time of day 0.

When it applies: at once.
