# World settings
@screen WorldSettingsScreen
Where: Options > World settings.

The open world's own settings, changed live. A world must be open.

What you can do here
- World border: centre, size, damage, safe zone, warning distance and time.
- Difficulty and Difficulty locked.
- Time of day (0 sunrise, 6000 noon, 12000 sunset, 18000 midnight) and day length in minutes.
- Weather and how long it lasts.
- Spawn angle.

Save sets only what you changed.

Examples
- A 2000-block world border centred on spawn.
- 40-minute days.
- Hard difficulty, locked.

When it applies: at once.
