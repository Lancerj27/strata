# Terrain
@screen OverridesScreen:terrain
Where: World > Terrain.

How the land is made: terrain settings, terrain shape, noises, and caves and canyons.

What you can do here
- Terrain settings: sea level, default block and fluid, surface rules.
- Terrain shape and noises: the math that makes hills and valleys.
- Caves and canyons: how often and how big they carve.

Examples
- Sea level at 80 for a flooded world.
- Deepslate as the default block.
- Fewer canyons: lower their chance.

When it applies: in a new world, or land not made yet.
