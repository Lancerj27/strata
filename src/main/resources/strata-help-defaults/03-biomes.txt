# Biomes
@screen BiomeListScreen
@screen BiomeEditScreen
@screen BiomeDataScreen
@screen OverridesScreen:biome
Where: World > Biomes.

Every biome of the game and its mods, grouped by tier. Biomes with no tier are in their own group.
A biome takes its tier's settings until you give it its own. A biome's own value always wins.

The biome list
- Search finds a biome by name or id.
- Edit, or a double-click, opens a biome.
- Columns show the biome's tier, level and more. Click a heading to change the columns.

The biome editor
- Level: the biome's own area level. Blank takes the tier's.
- Coefficient: the biome's own scaling multiplier. Blank takes the tier's.
- Mobs, Blocks and Structures open the biome's own lists. A biome list sits over its tier list.
- Rules holds the biome's spawn switches, over its tier's. See Tiers for what each rule does.
- Climate & looks edits the biome itself: temperature, downfall, rain or snow, sky, water and fog colors, grass and foliage colors, music, ambience, the mob spawns, and the carvers and features it places.
- Biome data: the chance a new chunk gets its animals, and each spawn cost's charge and energy budget.
- Data... opens biome placement (the climate ranges the world picks biomes with), biome modifiers (what a mod adds to biomes) and biome tags.

Climate & looks
This is the biome's own definition, as the game loads it.
- Select a field, type a value, Set value. Restore puts one field back.
- Features are grouped by step (ores, vegetation, lakes and so on). Select one and press Remove element to stop the biome placing it.
- Duplicate element copies a list member. Change the copy.
- A color is a whole number. The field's help line shows the game's value.

Step by step: a biome with its own level
1. World > Biomes. Search "plains". Edit Plains.
2. Type 5 in Level. Set value.
3. Plains is now level 5, even inside a level 1 tier.

Examples
- Stop rain in a biome: Climate & looks, Rain or snow, false.
- Change the grass color of a swamp: Climate & looks, its grass color.
- Take one ore out of one biome: Climate & looks, select the feature, Remove element.
- Give a modded biome a tier: open the tier, + Add biomes, search the mod's name.

When it applies
- Level and coefficient: at once.
- Climate & looks and Biome data: from the next world load. Land already made keeps its shape.
