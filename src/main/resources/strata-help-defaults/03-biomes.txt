# Biomes
@screen BiomeListScreen
@screen BiomeEditScreen
@screen BiomeDataScreen
@screen OverridesScreen:biome
Where: World > Biomes.

Every biome of the game and its mods, grouped by tier.
A biome takes its tier's settings until you give it its own.

What you can do here
- Edit opens a biome: its level, coefficient and own lists.
- From a tier's + Add biomes, Add shown to <tier> puts every biome the search shows into that tier.
- Mobs, Blocks and Structures open the biome's own lists.
- Rules holds the biome's spawn switches, over its tier's.
- Climate & looks edits the biome itself: temperature, rain, colours, music, ambience, and the carvers and features it places.
- Biome data: the chance a new chunk gets its animals, and each spawn cost's charge and energy budget.
- Data... opens biome placement (climate ranges), biome modifiers (a mod's additions) and biome tags.

Examples
- Make Plains a level 5 biome inside a level 1 tier.
- Stop rain in a biome: Climate & looks, Rain or snow, false.
- Change the grass colour of a swamp: Climate & looks, its grass colour.
- Take one ore out of one biome: Climate & looks, select the feature, Remove element.

When it applies
- Level and coefficient: at once.
- Climate & looks and Biome data: from the next world load. Land already made keeps its shape.
