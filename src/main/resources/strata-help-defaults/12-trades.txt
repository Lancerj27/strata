# Trades
@screen TradesScreen
@screen TradeEntryScreen
Where: the mob editor of a villager or the wandering trader, Trades.

One list per profession level (Novice, Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert, Master), and the wandering trader's common and rare lists.
At each level the game picks 2 trades from that level's list. The wandering trader picks 5 common and 1 rare.

The list
- Pick a profession at the top (for a villager), then a level.
- + Add trade adds a trade to the selected list.
- Edit opens a trade. Remove takes trades out.
- Restore takes back this scope's change on a game trade.

A trade
- Cost A and its count: what the player pays.
- Cost B and its count: a second price. 0 for none.
- Result and its count: what the player gets. Your item variants are listed first.
- Max uses: how many times before the trade locks until restock.
- XP: the XP the trader earns.
- Price multiplier: how much demand and reputation move the price.
- A game trade with a random part (an enchanted book, a map) can be removed. Editing it makes it a fixed trade.

Step by step: a Mending librarian
1. World > Mobs. Edit the villager. Trades.
2. Profession librarian. Level Master.
3. + Add trade. Cost A emerald, 30. Result enchanted book with Mending, 1. Max uses 3.
4. Save in Profiles.

Examples
- A farmer that buys pumpkins only at Master: remove the pumpkin trade at the lower levels.
- A wandering trader that sells your item variant: the rare list, + Add trade.
- Cheaper diamond tools: edit the toolsmith's trades, lower Cost A's count.

When it applies: to the next trader that reaches a level. Trades a trader already has stay.
Your first edit turns Options > Trade changes On, which takes effect at the next launch.
