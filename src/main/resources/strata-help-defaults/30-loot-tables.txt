# Loot tables
@screen ChestsScreen:tables
@screen LootTableScreen
Where: Items > Loot tables.

Every loot table that is not a chest's, a mob's or a block's: fishing, archaeology, gifts, bartering, and each mod's own.

What you can do here
- Edit opens the table's entries, pool by pool, each with its count and share of the picks.
- + Add loot entry, Edit and Remove change the entries.
- Edit on a pool's row sets how many times it picks.
- Loot table opens every raw value: conditions, enchantments and the rest.
- Restore all puts the whole table back.

Mob and block tables are on their own Loot screens. Options > Show duplicate editors lists them here too.

Examples
- Fishing gives more treasure.
- Piglins barter netherite scrap at 1%.
- A sniffer egg in suspicious sand.

When it applies: from the next world load.
