# Getting started
@screen ClientScreen
Strata changes how your world plays: which mobs spawn where, how strong they are, what they carry and drop, and the items, blocks and world generation of the game and its mods.

The main menu has three sections: World, Items and Blocks.
The dropdown at the title moves between a section's screens. Blocks has tabs across the top instead.
The buttons at the top right are on every screen:
- Go to: any World, Items or Blocks screen in one click.
- Profiles: save, load and switch configs.
- Scaling: mob scaling, player scaling and the formulas.
- Options: Strata's settings and tools.
- Exit: closes the editor.
- ?: the help for the screen you are on.

How the screens work
- Click a row to select it. Edit, or a double-click, opens it.
- Ctrl-click picks one more row. Shift-click picks a run of rows.
- Remove and On / Off act on every picked row.
- Yellow is a value you changed. Grey is switched off or locked.
- Restore puts back the value under yours. Restore all clears every change.
- Ctrl+Z, or Undo, takes back the last change.
- Click a group heading to open or fold it. A search shows every match.
- Click a column heading over a list to change, add or remove a column.

Examples
- Make zombies stronger deep underground: World > Tiers, make a tier, put the deep biomes in it, set its level.
- Give skeletons iron swords: World > Mobs, edit the skeleton, Equipment.
- Make diamonds rarer: Blocks > Ores, type a lower chance for diamond ore.
- Keep your work for the next launch: Profiles, Save.

Your edits save at once, but the next launch loads the active profile over them.
Press Save in Profiles to keep them.
