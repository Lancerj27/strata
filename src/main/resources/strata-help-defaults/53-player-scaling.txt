# Player scaling
@screen ScalingScreen:player
Where: Scaling > Player scaling.

How players grow stronger with their XP level.
scaled = base x (1 + per_level x level^exponent)
The base is the player's base stat. See Player base stats. The level is the player's XP level.

The screen
- The switch at the top turns player scaling on or off. It ships Off: the curves below grow players as soon as it is On.
- One row per attribute: max health, attack damage, attack speed, movement speed, armor, reach and more.
- Edit opens a row: per-level, exponent and start level.
- Player base stats opens the values the curves grow from.

A curve
- Per level: the bonus per level, as a fraction of base. 0.01 is +1% per level.
- Exponent: 1 is straight. Below 1 flattens the growth at high levels.
- Start level: the first level with a bonus.

Worked example
Max health 20. Per level 0.01, exponent 1. At XP level 50: 20 x (1 + 0.01 x 50) = 30 health, fifteen hearts.

Step by step: a slow climb
1. Scaling > Player scaling. Switch On.
2. Edit max health. Per level 0.01.
3. Edit attack damage. Per level 0.005. Start level 10.
4. Save in Profiles. Earn a level and check with /strata level get.

Examples
- +1 health per 5 levels: max health per_level 0.01 (on a base of 20).
- A little more attack speed per level, from level 20.
- Faster players at high level only: movement speed, exponent 2, per_level 0.0001.

When it applies: at once, after Save. Losing XP lowers the bonus again.
