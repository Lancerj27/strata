# Player scaling
@screen ScalingScreen:player
Where: Scaling > Player scaling.

How players grow stronger with their XP level.
scaled = base x (1 + per_level x level^exponent)

What you can do here
- One curve per attribute. Edit sets its per-level, exponent and start level.
- The switch at the top turns player scaling on or off. It ships Off: the curves below grow players as soon as it is On.
- Player base stats sets the values the curves grow from.

Examples
- +1 health per 5 levels.
- A little more attack speed per level, from level 20.

When it applies: at once, after Save.
