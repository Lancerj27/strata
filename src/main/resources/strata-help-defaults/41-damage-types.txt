# Damage types
@screen OverridesScreen:damage_types
Where: Options > Damage types.

Every kind of damage, and the damage tags, in one list.

What you can do here
- A damage type's exhaustion and how it scales with difficulty.
- Damage tags: what armor blocks, what bypasses shields, what burns.

Examples
- Fall damage that armor reduces.
- Arrows that ignore shields.

When it applies: at the next world load or /reload.
