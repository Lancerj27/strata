# Advancements
@screen OverridesScreen:advancement
Where: Options > Advancements.

Every advancement of the game and its mods.

What you can do here
- Its title, icon, rewards and criteria.
- Off: it cannot be earned.

Examples
- 100 XP for Diamonds!
- Hide the "How Did We Get Here?" advancement.

When it applies: at the next world load or /reload.
