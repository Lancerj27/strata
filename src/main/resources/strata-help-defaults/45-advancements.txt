# Advancements
@screen OverridesScreen:advancement
Where: Options > Advancements.

Every advancement of the game and its mods, grouped by tab (Minecraft, Nether, End, Adventure, Husbandry) and by mod.
Recipe advancements (the ones that unlock recipes) are here too.

What you can do here
- Display: title, description, icon, frame (task, goal, challenge), whether it shows a toast and announces in chat, and whether it is hidden.
- Parent: where it hangs in the tree.
- Criteria: what earns it, as JSON triggers. Requirements: which criteria together.
- Rewards: experience, loot tables, recipes and a function.
- Off: it cannot be earned. Its children stay but hang loose.
- Select a field, type a value, Set value. Restore puts it back.

Step by step: XP for diamonds
1. Search "diamonds". Select the Diamonds! advancement.
2. Rewards. Experience 100. Set value.
3. /reload.

Examples
- Hide the "How Did We Get Here?" advancement: Display, hidden true.
- A challenge frame on Stone Age.
- Your item variant as a reward: a loot table that gives it.

When it applies: at the next world load or /reload. Advancements already earned stay earned.
