# Jukebox songs
@screen OverridesScreen:jukebox_song
Where: Items > Data... > Jukebox songs.

Every music disc's song.

What you can do here
- A song's sound, length, name and the redstone signal it gives.

Examples
- A disc that plays your own sound (add it in Options > Custom textures & sounds first).
- A comparator signal of 15 for one disc.

When it applies: at the next world load or /reload.
