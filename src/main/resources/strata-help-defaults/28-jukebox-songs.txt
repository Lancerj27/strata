# Jukebox songs
@screen OverridesScreen:jukebox_song
Where: Items > Data... > Jukebox songs.

Every music disc's song: what plays, how long, and what a comparator reads.

What you can do here
- Sound event: the sound that plays. Pick the game's or one of your custom sounds.
- Length in seconds: how long the jukebox is busy, and when it drops the disc's particles.
- Description: the name on the disc.
- Comparator output: the redstone signal, 1 to 15.
- Select a field, type a value, Set value. Restore puts it back.

Step by step: your own song on a disc
1. Options > Custom textures & sounds. + Add custom... Sound. Browse... to your .ogg (at most 20 seconds). Name it.
2. Items > Data... > Jukebox songs. Select a disc's song, for example 13.
3. Sound event, pick your sound. Length in seconds, type its length. Set value.
4. /reload. The disc plays your song.

Examples
- A comparator signal of 15 for one disc.
- A different name for a disc.

When it applies: at the next world load or /reload.
