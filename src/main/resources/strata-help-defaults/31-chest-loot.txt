# Chest loot
@screen ChestsScreen:chests
@screen ChestLootScreen
@screen ChestPoolScreen
@screen ChestEntryScreen
Where: Items > Chest loot.

The loot in the chests of villages, dungeons, shipwrecks, bastions and every other structure, grouped by structure.

What you can do here
- Edit opens a chest's entries, pool by pool.
- + Add loot entry puts an item into the selected pool, with its weight and count.
- Edit on a pool's row sets how many times it picks.
- Remove: your entry goes; a game entry gets weight 0. Restore brings it back.
- Restore all puts the whole table back.

Examples
- Diamonds in village blacksmith chests.
- No enchanted golden apples in ruined portals.
- Your item variant in end city chests.

When it applies: from the next world load. Chests not yet opened get the new loot too.
