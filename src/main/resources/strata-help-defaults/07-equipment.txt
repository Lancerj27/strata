# Equipment
@screen EquipmentScreen
@screen EquipmentItemScreen
Where: the mob editor, Equipment.

What the mob wears and holds, slot by slot: main hand, off hand, head, chest, legs and feet.

The list
- One row per item in a slot. A slot may hold several rows: the game picks one by chance.
- + Add adds a row. Pick the slot, then the item. Your item variants are listed first.
- Edit opens a row. Remove deletes the picked rows.
- Deny empties a slot: the mob never holds anything there.
- Restore all removes your rows. The game's equipment comes back.
- Local difficulty Yes: the chances drop in new or easy areas, as the game's armor does. No: the chance is the same everywhere.

A row
- Item: what it holds or wears.
- Chance on Normal: the percent chance the row is used.
- Chance on Hard: the chance on Hard difficulty. A blank Hard is the Normal chance.
- Drop %: the chance it drops when a player kills the mob.
- Enchant %: the chance the item is enchanted. The level follows local difficulty.
- Scaled: the chance grows with the area level.
- Player level: a range of player XP levels the row counts for, like 10-20 or 30+.

How rows add up
A slot you give a row is yours: a bow at 25% is a 25% bow and a 75% empty hand.
A slot with no row keeps the game's equipment.
A tier's mob takes the global rows, and a variant its base mob's, until you save its own.
Worn armor adds to the mob's own armor. Scaling raises the mob's own stat, not the armor.

Step by step: armored zombies on Hard
1. Edit the zombie. Equipment.
2. + Add. Slot Head. Diamond helmet. Normal 0, Hard 50.
3. Repeat for Chest, Legs and Feet.
4. Edit each row. Drop % 5.
5. Save in Profiles.

Examples
- Skeletons with a 10% chance of an iron sword.
- A boss variant whose helmet always drops: Drop % 100.
- Zombies with no armor at all: Deny on each armor slot.
- Your "Frostbite" sword on a stray: + Add, Main hand, pick the variant.

When it applies: to mobs that spawn after the save.
