# Equipment
@screen EquipmentScreen
@screen EquipmentItemScreen
Where: the mob editor, Equipment.

What the mob wears and holds, slot by slot.

What you can do here
- Add an item to a slot with a chance on Normal and on Hard. A blank Hard is the Normal chance.
- Drop %: the chance it drops when a player kills the mob. Enchant %: the chance it is enchanted.
- Deny empties a slot. A slot with no row keeps the game's equipment.
- Local difficulty Yes: the chances drop in new or easy areas, as the game's armor does.
- Restore all removes your rows.

A slot you give a row is yours: a bow at 25% is a 25% bow and a 75% empty hand.
A tier's mob takes the global rows, and a variant its base mob's, until you save its own.
Worn armor adds to the mob's own armor. Scaling raises the mob's own stat, not the armor.

Examples
- Skeletons with a 10% chance of an iron sword.
- Zombies in full diamond armor on Hard only.
- A boss variant whose helmet always drops: Drop % 100.

When it applies: to mobs that spawn after the save.
