# Vegetation
@screen VegetationScreen
Where: Blocks > Vegetation, or the Blocks button of a tier or biome.

The plants, trees, flowers, mushrooms and other growth the world places, on or off.
A biome's rule wins over its tier's, a tier's over Global.

The list
- One row per vegetation feature, grouped by kind: trees, flowers, grass, mushrooms, crops, water plants and a mod's.
- On / Off for each.
- Default: the row takes the scope beneath.
- Search finds one by name.

Step by step: a dead land
1. World > Tiers. Edit the "Wasteland" tier. Blocks. Vegetation tab.
2. Select every tree and flower row (Shift-click a run). Off.
3. Save in Profiles.

Examples
- No tall grass in a desert-like tier.
- No cherry trees in one biome.
- Mushrooms everywhere: brown and red mushrooms On at Global.

For how many and how big, see Features. For one biome's own list, see Biomes, Climate & looks.

When it applies: in land made after the next world load.
