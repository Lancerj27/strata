# Vegetation
@screen VegetationScreen
Where: Blocks > Vegetation, or the Blocks button of a tier or biome.

The plants, trees and flowers, on or off.
A biome's rule wins over its tier's, a tier's over Global.

What you can do here
- On / Off for each plant, tree or flower.

Examples
- No tall grass in a desert-like tier.
- No cherry trees in one biome.

When it applies: in land made after the next world load.
