# Items
@screen ItemsScreen
@screen ItemStatScreen
Where: Items > Items.

Every item of the game and its mods, by kind: weapons, armor, tools, food, blocks and the rest.
Your item variants sit under their item.

What you can do here
- Edit opens the item's values, each with what it does and the game's number: stack size, durability, rarity, fire resistance, food, mining speed, attack and armor.
- Every other component is a value too (lore, repair cost, potion contents and more), shown as JSON.
- + Add component gives the item any component the game or a mod has.
- The crafting grid on the item's page shows the recipes that make it. + Add recipe makes your own. Shaped: the pattern counts. Shapeless: only the items count.
- On / Off disables the picked items: they leave the creative tabs, recipes that make them switch off, and no loot drops them.
- + Add variant, under an item, makes your own item from it.
- Data... opens recipes, brewing, enchantments, armor trims, banner patterns, jukebox songs, paintings, item tags and item data.

Examples
- Swords with 3000 durability.
- Ender pearls that stack to 64.
- Bread that fills more hunger.
- A recipe for saddles from leather and iron.
- Disable the elytra.

When it applies
- Item values: at the next game start. The server and each player need the same profile.
- Recipes and disabling: at the next world load or /reload.
