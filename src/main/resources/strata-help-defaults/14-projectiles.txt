# Projectiles
@screen ProjectileListScreen
@screen ProjectileEditScreen
Where: World > Mobs > Data... > Projectiles.

Your own projectiles, fired as one the game or a mod has: an arrow, a snowball, a fireball, a trident.

What you can do here
- Speed, gravity (0 flies straight, 1 falls as normal), damage, explosion power and seconds of fire.
- Looks like item draws it as any item. Arrow texture draws an arrow with any texture.
- Keep its own impact: Yes still does its own thing where it lands (a fireball's blast).
- Abilities: what happens where it lands, the same abilities a mob has.
- Off fires the plain projectile it is based on.

A mob fires one with the Shoots a projectile goal, or Combat in Mob data.

Examples
- A snowball that deals 4 damage and slows the target.
- A straight-flying arrow with gravity 0.
- A fireball that explodes with power 2 and sets fire for 5 seconds.

When it applies: from the next shot.
