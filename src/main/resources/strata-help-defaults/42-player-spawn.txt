# Player spawn
@screen PlayerSpawnScreen
Where: Options > Player spawn.

Move the world spawn to a biome or a tier, for testing a start.

What you can do here
- Pick a biome or a tier: the world spawn moves to the nearest place of it, and you with it.
- Off stops the moves.

Examples
- Start every test in a desert.
- Start in your hardest tier.

When it applies: at once.
