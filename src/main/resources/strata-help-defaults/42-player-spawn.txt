# Player spawn
@screen PlayerSpawnScreen
Where: Options > Player spawn.

Move the world spawn to a biome or a tier, for testing a start.
Strata searches around the current spawn for the nearest place of that biome or tier, moves the world spawn there, and moves you with it.

What you can do here
- Biome: pick a biome. The spawn moves to the nearest one.
- Tier: pick a tier. The spawn moves to the nearest biome of it.
- Off stops the moves. The spawn stays where it last moved.
- Search radius (blocks), in Settings: how far Strata looks. A rare biome needs more.

Step by step: start in your hardest tier
1. Options > Player spawn.
2. Tier. Pick "Deep".
3. You and the world spawn move to the nearest deep biome. Die, and you respawn there.

Examples
- Start every test in a desert.
- Check a biome's looks fast: pick it, look, pick the next.

When it applies: at once in an open world, and when any world starts.
