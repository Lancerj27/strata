# Mobs
@screen MobListScreen
@screen MobBatchScreen
@screen MobCapScreen
@screen MobBiomesScreen
@screen ReplacementsScreen
Where: World > Mobs.

Every mob of the game and its mods, grouped by category: Monster, Creature, Ambient, Water creature, Water ambient, Underground water creature, Axolotls and Misc.
The tabs show the Global list, a tier's list or a biome's list.
Your variants sit under their base mob.

The mob list
- Edit, or a double-click, opens the mob editor. See Mob editor.
- + Add mob puts a mob on this tier's or biome's spawn list. Pick the mob, then type its weight and group size.
- Edit picked changes every picked mob at once: spawning, scaling, mob griefing, category, loot, spawn rules, abilities or equipment.
- Remove takes mobs off this list. On a mob the game spawns here it reads Restore: the game's own spawn comes back.
- Add default mobs adds the mobs the game spawns here as rows, with the game's numbers. Edit the rows to change the game's own spawns.
- On / Off stops the picked mobs spawning here, and keeps their rows.
- Replace mob swaps one mob for another where it spawns.
- Mob caps: how many mobs of each category an area holds, and how far away one may despawn.
- Special spawns opens the game's special spawners. See Special spawns.
- Goal sets opens the shared AI lists. See Goals and goal sets.
- Data... opens projectiles, cat and wolf variants, mob tags and mob data maps.
- Click a column heading to show health, damage, speed, weight or another stat.

Scopes
- The Global tab is every mob in the world.
- A tier tab is the tier's own spawn list. In a Replace tier, only this list spawns.
- A biome tab is the biome's own list, over its tier's.
- A yellow value at a scope is the scope's own. A white value is inherited.

Replace mob
- Replace: the mob that would spawn. With: the mob that spawns.
- Share %: how many change. Blank is 100. At 50, half the zombies are husks.
- Sources natural: only the biome's own spawns. Sources all: structures, spawners and patrols too.
- Eggs and commands are never changed.
- Replacements never chain: a mob a replacement made is not replaced again.

Mob caps
- One row per category: the cap and the despawn distances.
- The cap is per player, counted in the chunks around the player, as the game does.
- Lower a cap for fewer mobs. Raise it for more. Blank is the game's number.

Step by step: a desert full of husks
1. World > Tiers. Make a "Desert" tier with the desert biomes.
2. World > Mobs. Open the tier's tab.
3. + Add mob. Pick the husk. Weight 80, Min 2, Max 4.
4. Save in Profiles. Walk into a desert after the next world load.

Examples
- Replace 30% of zombies with your "Brute" variant in a dark forest.
- Lower the monster cap so fewer monsters crowd the night.
- Set many mobs to the Monster category at once with Edit picked.
- Stop bats everywhere: pick the bat on Global, Off.

When it applies
- Stats and mob caps: at once.
- Spawn lists and natural replacements: from the next world load.
- Replacements for all sources: at once.
