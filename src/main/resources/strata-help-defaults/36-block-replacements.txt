# Block replacements
@screen BlockSwapScreen
Where: Blocks > Block replacements, or the Blocks button of a tier or biome.

Where the world would make the From block, it makes the To block, or nothing (air) when To is Disabled.
A biome's rule wins over its tier's, a tier's over Global. Rules never chain: Stone to Deepslate and Deepslate to Tuff leaves the stone as deepslate.

The list
- One row per rule: From, To, and the scope the rule comes from.
- + Add, then pick From and To. Your block variants are listed first in To.
- Edit changes a rule. Remove takes rules out.
- Disabled as To removes the block from new land.

What it covers
- The land itself: stone, dirt, sand, the surface.
- Ores and features the world places.
- Structures, as they are placed.
- Not builds, and not blocks a player places.

Step by step: a deepslate underworld
1. World > Tiers. Edit the "Deep" tier. Blocks. Block replacements tab.
2. + Add. From Stone. To Deepslate.
3. Save in Profiles. New deep chunks are deepslate.

Examples
- Grass Block to Dirt in Plains.
- No gravel anywhere: Gravel to Disabled.
- Your block variant as the ground: Stone to strata:hard_stone.
- Sand to red sand in a tier.

When it applies: to chunks made from now on. Land already made keeps its blocks. A regenerate (Profiles) applies it to old chunks too.
