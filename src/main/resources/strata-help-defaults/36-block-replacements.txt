# Block replacements
@screen BlockSwapScreen
Where: Blocks > Block replacements, or the Blocks button of a tier or biome.

Where the world would make the From block, it makes the To block, or nothing (air) when To is Disabled.
A biome's rule wins over its tier's, a tier's over Global. Rules never chain.

What you can do here
- + Add, then pick From and To.
- Remove takes rules out.

Examples
- Grass Block to Dirt in Plains.
- Stone to Deepslate in a deep tier.
- No gravel anywhere: Gravel to Disabled.

When it applies: to chunks made from now on. Land already made keeps its blocks.
