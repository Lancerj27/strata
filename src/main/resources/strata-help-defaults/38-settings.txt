# Settings
@screen StrataOptionsScreen
Where: Options > Settings.

Strata's own switches and this machine's preferences.

What you can do here
- Spawn substitution, ore restriction and loot overrides: switch each part of Strata on or off.
- Editor key: the key that opens Strata.
- GUI scale: Auto, Game, or a size.
- Show locked values: lists values Strata cannot change, greyed.
- Show duplicate editors: lists things another screen edits too.
- Server textures & sounds: Ask, Always or Never.
- Debug HUD: shows the biome, tier, area level and your XP level in the corner.
- Special spawners, Trade changes and Extra mob attributes: Strata's own versions of the game's spawners,
  trade lists and mob attributes. They ship Off, so the game is its own until you edit. A Special spawns or
  Trades edit turns its switch On. Turn Extra mob attributes On for attack goals and Flying on mobs the game
  gives no attack damage or flying speed.

Examples
- Turn off ore restriction to test with the game's ores.
- The Debug HUD to check which tier you stand in.

When it applies: at once.
