# Textures
@screen AssetMasterScreen:Texture
Where: Options > Textures.

Every texture the game loaded, grouped by mod and folder.

What you can do here
- Replace... puts one of your custom textures in its place. Keep the original's size.
- Restore puts the game's texture back.

Examples
- A new texture for diamond ore.
- A different zombie skin for every zombie.

Replacements are saved in the profile. Load active profile to server (in Profiles) sends them to every player.
