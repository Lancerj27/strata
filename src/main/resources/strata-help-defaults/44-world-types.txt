# World types
@screen OverridesScreen:world_types
Where: Options > World types.

The world types and superflat layouts a new world can be made as, and their tags.

What you can do here
- A world type's dimensions and generators.
- A superflat layout's layers, biome and structures.
- The tags decide which types and layouts the create-world screen shows.

Examples
- A superflat layout of 60 stone and a grass layer.
- Hide the Amplified world type.

When it applies: to worlds made after the change.
