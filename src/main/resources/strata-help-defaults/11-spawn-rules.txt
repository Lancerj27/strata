# Spawn rules
@screen SpawnRulesScreen
Where: the mob editor, Spawn rules.

Conditions a mob must meet to spawn here.

What you can do here
- + Add rule picks a condition and its value:
  - Time of day: day, night, dusk, dawn, or a tick range.
  - Light: 0 (dark) to 15, a number or a range like 0-7.
  - Height: a number or a range like -64-0.
  - Weather: clear, rain, thunder or not clear.
  - Moon phase: 0 (full moon) to 7.
  - Structure: only inside a structure.
  - Surface or underground.
  - Distance from the world spawn, like 100-500 or 500+.
  - Days since a player last slept, the world's day, or the nearest player's level.
- Remove takes a rule out.

The game's own checks always hold too: a monster needs darkness, an animal grass and light, a fish water. A rule only narrows them.

Examples
- Zombies only below Y 0.
- Phantoms only on a new moon.
- Strong mobs only 1000 blocks or more from spawn.
- Slimes only in rain.
- Pillager patrols from day 1: change the pillager's World day rule from 5+ to 1+.

When it applies: at once.
