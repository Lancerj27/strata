# Blocks
@screen BlockStatsScreen
@screen BlockStatScreen
Where: Blocks > Blocks.

Every block of the game and its mods, by kind: ores, stone and metal, wood, plants, liquids and the rest.
The tabs at the top open Ores, Vegetation and Block replacements.

What you can do here
- Edit opens a block's values: hardness, blast resistance, the correct-tool rule, friction, speed and jump, light level, flammability, sound, map colour, collision and more.
- On / Off disables the picked blocks: they cannot be placed, leave the creative tabs, and stop generating.
- + Add variant, under a block, makes your own block from it.
- Data... opens block tags, block data (waxing, oxidizing), fluid tags, game event tags and workstation tags.

Examples
- Obsidian that breaks in 2 seconds.
- Ice that is not slippery: friction 0.6.
- Glowstone with light level 10.
- Disable the TNT block.

When it applies: at once. A new light level shows where light is worked out again.
