# Mob editor
@screen MobEditScreen
Where: World > Mobs, Edit on a mob.

One mob, or one of your variants, at the scope you opened it from: Global, a tier or a biome.
The title names the mob and the scope.

Spawning
- Spawn weight: its share when the game picks among its category's mobs. Not a chance. A zombie at 100 beside a skeleton at 100 is a 50% zombie.
- Min count and Max count: the group size when it spawns.
- Spawn chance %: how often a picked spawn then happens. Blank is 100.

Scaling
- Scaling: whether this mob scales with the area level.
- Coefficient: multiplies its scaling bonus. Blank is 1.
- Start level: the first level with a bonus. Below it the mob is its base self.

Identity
- Display name, for a variant. The game shows it over the mob and in death messages.
- Goal set: which AI list it runs. See Goals and goal sets.

The sub-screens
- Mob data: every value the game holds for it. See Mob data.
- Biomes: which biomes this mob's row counts in, when the row is on a tier.
- Spawn rules: conditions for a spawn. See Spawn rules.
- Loot: extra drops and its loot table. See Mob loot.
- Equipment: what it wears and holds. See Equipment.
- Abilities: special powers on a trigger. See Abilities.
- Trades: for villagers and the wandering trader. See Trades.
- Category, Mob griefing and Spawn as are in Mob data, at the top of Attributes.
- Restore all puts the mob back as the game makes it, in every scope. A list of what goes comes first.

Variants
- A variant is your own mob made from a base mob, with its own name, look and values.
- + Add variant on the mob list, under a mob, makes one. Type a name of lowercase letters, digits and _.
- It uses its base mob's values for anything it does not set itself.
- Give the skeleton a sword and its variants carry it too.
- A variant spawns only where you put it on a list. It has no spawns of its own.

Step by step: a frost zombie
1. World > Mobs. Select the zombie. + Add variant. Name "frost_zombie".
2. Edit the variant. Display name "Frost Zombie".
3. Mob data > Visuals. Tint, pick a blue. Or Texture, pick one of your custom textures.
4. Mob data > Attributes. Max health 40.
5. Abilities. + Add. Apply effect, slowness, on hit, 100%.
6. Back on the mob list, open a cold tier's tab. + Add mob, pick frost_zombie, weight 50.

Examples
- Zombies that spawn in groups of 4 to 6.
- A rare golden skeleton: a variant with weight 2 and better loot.
- A zombie that does not scale: Scaling Off.

When it applies: at once, after Save. Spawn weight and counts reach the spawn lists from the next world load.
