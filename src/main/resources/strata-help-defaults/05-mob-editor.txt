# Mob editor
@screen MobEditScreen
Where: World > Mobs, Edit on a mob.

One mob, or one of your variants, at the scope you opened it from: Global, a tier or a biome.

What you can do here
- Spawn weight: its share when the game picks among its category's mobs (not a chance).
- Min count and Max count: the group size.
- Spawn chance %: how often it then spawns. Blank is 100.
- Scaling, Coefficient and Start level: whether and how strongly it scales, and the first level with a bonus.
- Display name, for a variant.
- Goal set: which AI it runs.
- Mob data, Biomes, Spawn rules, Loot, Equipment, Abilities and Trades open their own screens.
- Category, Mob griefing and Spawn as are in Mob data, at the top of Attributes.
- Restore all puts the mob back as the game makes it, in every scope. A list of what goes comes first.

Variants
- A variant is your own mob made from a base mob, with its own name, look and values.
- It uses its base mob's values for anything it does not set itself.
- Give the skeleton a sword and its variants carry it too.

Examples
- A "Frost Zombie" variant: blue tint, slowness on hit, double health.
- Zombies that spawn in groups of 4 to 6.
- A rare golden skeleton: a variant with weight 2 and better loot.

When it applies: at once, after Save. Spawn weight and counts reach the spawn lists from the next world load.
